      Tournament setup

      Roster & presentation

      All student Python files stay in f1/ and f2/. Each class manifest selects its own roster.

      Classes and their strategies are configured in f1/manifest.json and f2/manifest.json.

      No official strategies loaded.
      Tournament rules

      Tournament 1 fixes 100 rounds, full history, and multiplier 1.0. Each cross-faction pairing plays once.

      The multiplier changes only mutual Keep: each side receives 2 × multiplier. The payoff matrix is revealed on launch.

      Winner-stays play order

      Arrange each faction from first to last. The winner stays and the next surviving opponent follows this order. The match selectors also let you choose a trial pair.
