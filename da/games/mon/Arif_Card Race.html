Card Race

Race all 4 stones around the track, then up or down your own lane (F1 to F4) to the GOAL. Each card moves a stone that many boxes. Card 1 opens a stone from Home, or moves 1. Card 4 goes back 4. Card 6 moves your stone 6, or a rival stone 6. Card 9 moves two stones 9 each. Land on a rival stone to capture it. You need the exact number to reach the GOAL. Watch the special boxes!
You cannot land on your own stone, or on an occupied Safe Star. Open square rules: a stone sitting on its own OPEN square blocks rivals, who cannot land on it or pass it. A rival stone sitting on your OPEN square is captured when you open a stone with Card 1.
Player 1 = round coral stones (bottom). Player 2 = square mint stones (top).
