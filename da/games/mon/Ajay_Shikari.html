How to Play

🎯 AimDrag the pouch backward, then release. Pull farther for more power.
🌬️ Read the windWind nudges stones sideways. The HUD shows its direction and strength.
🐦 Match colorsHit only birds matching the remaining objective colors.
✦ Master StoneTap its button, then tap a required bird for a guaranteed hit.

Two unsuccessful shots in a row fail the level. Obstacles cost a stone. Finish quickly for bonus coins and a Master Stone.

Desktop: mouse drag. Mobile: touch drag. Escape pauses. This game is a stylized fictional arcade tribute, not a hunting simulator.

Credits & Notes

SHIKARI (शिखारी) is an original single-file browser game concept inspired by Himalayan landscapes, Nepali folk instruments, and the traditional guleli.

Birds and scenery are procedurally illustrated with Canvas. The five soundtrack styles and sound effects are synthesized locally using Web Audio API. No external assets, libraries, accounts, or network services are required.

Created as a student portfolio project. Species-inspired silhouettes are stylized rather than scientific illustrations.
