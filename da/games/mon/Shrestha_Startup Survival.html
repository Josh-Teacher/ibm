Welcome to Startup Survival! 🏪

You have 30 days to turn a small neighborhood shop into a thriving business. Every decision matters: customers get impatient, deliveries take time, and daily expenses keep coming.

🧾 Serve customers
Stay at the counter. Customers bring their goods; select them and check out.
📦 Restock fast
Regular orders arrive in 2 days; Quick +3 adds stock now for 50% extra.
💲 Choose prices
Higher prices can reduce demand.
🎯 Win your way
Survive all 30 days for a performance ending.

Starting cash is $1,000. Your first stock investment costs $76, leaving $924. Daily operating expenses are $20. Each day ends after about 60 seconds, or sooner when you press End Day. The game pauses while a screen is open.
