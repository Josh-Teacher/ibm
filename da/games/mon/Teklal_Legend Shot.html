Field guide

HOW TO PLAY

Aim first. Charge while you fine-tune your aim. Release to let the axe fly.

Controls

Mouse: Move to aim; hold the left button to charge and release to throw. Keep moving while charging.

Keyboard: Arrow keys aim; hold Space to charge and release Space to throw. P or Escape pauses. R restarts the run.

Touch: Touch the arena to begin charging, drag to adjust aim, then lift to throw.

Wind & power

The arrow shows where wind pushes the axe. Aim against it. Wind stays steady through a throw and can strengthen as your score rises.

Charge tops out after 1.8 seconds. Power multiplies points from 1.0× to 2.0×, but control error also grows with charge.

The dotted circle shows possible control error; it is uncertainty, not a guaranteed landing zone.
