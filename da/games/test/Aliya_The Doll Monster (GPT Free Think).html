Investigator's Instructions

Mission: Find the clues, reconnect the right wires, recover and equip the gas mask, defeat the doll with the gas system, then rescue both children.

Move & interactW A S D or arrow keys to move. E inspects nearby objects, searches drawers, hides, equips, presses the button, or rescues.
Explore for cluesNotes and labels reveal the correct cable symbol. Use the Investigation Log to review clues you've found.
Electrical panelsClick one of the three cables. A wrong choice makes a dangerous noise and starts a hunt. The right choice solves the puzzle.
Hide from the dollApproach a marked hiding spot and press E. Stay hidden while the doll searches for exactly 3 seconds. Leave after it departs.
Mask & gasSearch drawers in Station Two. Find and explicitly equip the mask before using the second panel or pressing “PUSH HERE.”
Lives & scoringStart with 3 lives. Death costs 1 life and restarts the current station's tasks. Scores persist; earn extra lives at 300 and 600 points. Losing all lives resets the mission.

Dangerous electrical mistakes attract the monster. Ordinary walking and drawer searches are silent. No weapons exist; poisonous gas is the only way to defeat the doll. Both children must be rescued to win.
