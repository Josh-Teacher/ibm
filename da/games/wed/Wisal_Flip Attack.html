FLIP ATTACK

Red vs Blue on a 6×6 battlefield. 25 turns.

How to Play

  • Goal: Finish 25 total turns with a higher score than your opponent. Red moves first, then players alternate.
  • Place: Click, tap, or use Tab + Enter/Space on an empty cell. Placing a piece gives +1.
  • Capture: Your new piece and another piece of yours can trap a straight line of opponent pieces. All trapped pieces turn to your colour.
  • 8 directions: Up, down, left, right and the 4 diagonals. Several directions can capture in the same turn.
  • Scoring: Place +1 · Capture +2 per piece · Bonus +3 · Bomb +4 · Shield +1.
  • ⭐ Bonus Cell: +3 points.
  • 💣 Bomb Cell: Choose one neighbouring opponent piece and flip it (+4). No target means no points.
  • 🛡️ Shield Cell: +1 point. Your piece can't be flipped on the opponent's next turn.
  • ×2 Double Score Cell: Your next successful capture gives double capture points (placement and special points are not doubled).
  • Special cells are in fixed places and work only once.
  • Winner: Highest score after Turn 25. Tie: more pieces wins. Still tied: draw.
