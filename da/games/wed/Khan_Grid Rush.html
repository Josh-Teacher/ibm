GRID RUSH

A number is called every 10 seconds. Find it on your own board and tap it before the next call. Both players race at the same time.

  • Win with a row, column, diagonal or the four corners.
  • Wrong taps are ignored. Numbers missing from your board cost nothing.
  • Every 3 correct marks, your next matching number glows gold for 2 seconds.
