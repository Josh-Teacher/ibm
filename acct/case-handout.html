Summit Trail Co.

Weeks 1–7 and 9–12 · Outdoor services and equipment retail · June

Assumptions

  • New corporation; all beginning balances are zero.
  • Perpetual inventory with supplied costs of goods sold.
  • The note requires $500 monthly principal payments. At June 30, $6,000 is due in the next 12 months.
  • Prepaid insurance and customer services begin in July.
  • No adjusting or closing entries; depreciation and other period-end estimates are outside this simplified exercise.
  • Interest paid is operating; dividends and loan principal paid are financing.
